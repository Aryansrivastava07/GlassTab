Update GlassTab

Checking the latest build…

  1. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
  2. Find GlassTab and check its loaded folder in Details.
  3. Click the button and select that same folder. Your browser will request permission to update its files.
  4. When finished, click Reload on the extension card and open a new tab.

Your notes and settings are stored in browser storage; this updater writes release files only. Existing extra files in the folder are left in place.
